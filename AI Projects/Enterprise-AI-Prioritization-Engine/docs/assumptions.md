# Scenario assumptions

Reviewed 13 September 2026. The registry retains the original numeric coefficients only as hypothetical teaching inputs. There is no supporting rate-card or carbon-measurement provenance. No provider/model is represented by these scenarios.

Cost units: USD per million tokens. Carbon units: kg CO2e per million tokens. Excludes input/output/cache pricing differences, region, hardware, utilisation, other services and lifecycle emissions. Do not use this arithmetic as a vendor quotation, measured footprint or net-zero assessment.

Priority weights (60% subjective impact, 40% subjective feasibility) and the 65 quadrant threshold are design choices. Test sensitivity to different weights and stakeholder ratings before using recommendations. No realised ROI or business uplift is inferred.
